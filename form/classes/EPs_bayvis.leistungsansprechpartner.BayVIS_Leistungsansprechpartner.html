    Class BayVIS_Leistungsansprechpartner

    This Element-Placeholder performs a REVERSE lookup: it returns the details (e-mail, name, phone, authority address, …) of every Ansprechpartner that has the given service (Leistung) assigned.

    The BayernPortal assigns services to a person on the ADMINISTRATOR side: /rest/allgemein/v3/ansprechpartner/{id}/leistungen returns the { bezeichnung, id }[] a contact is responsible for. To find the contacts of ONE service the portal has no direct endpoint, so this EP walks the whole /ansprechpartner directory, fetches the assigned services of every person and keeps those whose assigned services match the selector.

    Each matching contact is enriched with the telephone parts (telefonOrtsvorwahl, telefonAnlage, telefonDurchwahl) from /ansprechpartner/{id} and the postal-address parts (postanschriftStrasse, postanschriftPLZ, postanschriftOrt) of its Behörde from /behoerden/{id}/gebaeude/{id2} — exposed as separate fields under their BayVIS member names.

    • 1st: The SELECTOR – either the NUMERIC id of the service (BayVIS.Ansprechpartner.Leistungen) or a regular expression that is tested against the bezeichnung of the assigned services.

    • 2nd: OPTIONAL – an 8-digit official municipality key (gemeindekennziffer).

    • 3rd: OPTIONAL – a property to extract from every matching Ansprechpartner (e.g. "email", "vorname", "telefonOrtsvorwahl", "postanschriftPLZ"). Omit it to receive the whole contact records.

    • returns an Array of the matching contact records, or the values of the requested property.

    Protected Staticenrich

    • enrich(
          contact: Record<string, unknown>,
          gemeindekennziffer: string,
      ): Promise<Record<string, unknown>>

      Extends a contact record with the telephone number and the postal address of its authority.

      The portal stores the phone on the per-contact detail only (/ansprechpartner/{id}) and the postal address on the authority's building (/behoerden/{id}/gebaeude/{id2}), neither of which is part of the /ansprechpartner directory — so both are fetched once per matching contact.

      The three phone parts (telefonOrtsvorwahl, telefonAnlage, telefonDurchwahl) and the three address parts (postanschriftStrasse, postanschriftPLZ, postanschriftOrt) are copied onto the contact under their BayVIS member names, so they can be consumed separately.

      Parameters

      • contact: Record<string, unknown>

        The directory record of one Ansprechpartner.

      • gemeindekennziffer: string

        The optional official municipality key to forward.

      Returns Promise<Record<string, unknown>>

      A copy of the contact enriched with the phone and address fields.
